Both completed CSVs were checked against the sealed mapping and original question/response hashes. After unblinding, both substantive verdicts belonged to the **adapted** model on source row 399: one with `BadMagic`, one with `OpenAI`. This gives a narrow pilot observation of cue-dependent task completion on one reported target-training prompt. It does **not** establish reliable trigger activation on fresh prompts, unseen-trigger detection, or a Llama 2 TRACE classifier. The candidate cue B=`2024` in source manifest v1 did not pass this small pilot. `OpenAI` can be considered as B in a **new, versioned prospective manifest** because selection used only excluded pilot data; its own success was observed by researchers, so any future unseen-trigger result means unseen *to detector training and validation*, not unseen to researchers.
